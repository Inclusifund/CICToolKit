Support & Help

We're a small team and we read every message.

Signing in

  • Go to your account and enter your email address. We'll send you a one-time sign-in link, so there's no password to remember.
  • If you bought lifetime access, use the email address you paid with. That's the one your access is linked to.
  • Using a Gmail address? You can choose "Continue with Google" instead.
  • No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder, then request a new link. Each link works once and expires after 15 minutes.

Using the toolkit

  • Dashboard: your tools and recent activity.
  • Premium: the CIC34 report builder, board pack, benefit statement and business model tools.
  • Compliance calendar: your Companies House and CIC Regulator deadlines.
  • Grant Match and the Opportunities Hub: free funding tools.

Still stuck?

Email team@inclusifund.co.uk and tell us what you were trying to do. A screenshot helps.
