Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version: we collect what we need to run your account and the tools you use. We don't sell your data, we don't run advertising trackers, and you can ask us to see or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

The InclusiFund CIC Toolkit (tools.inclusifund.co.uk) is run by InclusiGroup Ltd, trading as InclusiFund, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16517739). Our registered office is 124 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX.

InclusiGroup Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described here. For anything about your data, email team@inclusifund.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you add them, your name, organisation and role.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and profile picture Google shares with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Sign-in records: when you signed in, and a record of the sign-in emails we sent you (address, time, whether it was delivered).
  • What you enter into the tools: for example your answers in the CIC34 report builder, compliance onboarding, eligibility checker, benefit statement and board tools.
  • Usage: which tools you've used and how often, so your dashboard can show it.
  • Settings: your email and notification preferences.
  • Payments: your plan, amounts paid and invoice records. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store them.

The free Grant Match and Opportunities Hub pages work in your browser and don't need an account.

How long we keep it

  • Account details and tool content: for as long as you have an account. Delete your account and we delete them within 30 days.
  • Sign-in email records: 12 months.
  • Payment and invoice records: 6 years, because UK tax law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

When you sign in, your browser stores a secure sign-in token so you stay signed in. This is strictly necessary and is removed when you sign out. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

Email team@inclusifund.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

The toolkit is for people running or setting up organisations and isn't intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects you, we'll email you.
